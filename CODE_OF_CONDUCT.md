# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Contributors must communicate respectfully, criticize ideas rather than people, distinguish evidence
from interpretation, disclose uncertainty, and make room for correction. Harassment, intimidation,
discrimination, doxxing, and deliberate scientific misrepresentation are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed
confidentially and proportionately. Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

This project follows the spirit and enforcement ladder of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
